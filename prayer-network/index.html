Prayer Is the Foundation

The Prayer Network

Open to men and women. A simple, steady practice of carrying one another's needs to God, three times a week.

"The prayer of a righteous person is powerful and effective."
James 5:16
Why Prayer
Everything else runs on this

TTP exists to form men. But formation does not come from effort alone. The triad, the arms, the retreats -- all of it rests on a foundation that has to be prayer. Not as a formality. Not as an item on a list. As the actual engine.

The Prayer Network is the most concrete expression of that conviction. A network of people who have committed to carrying each other's needs to God, three times a week, without exception.

Who It Is For
Open to all

TPN is not restricted to TTP members. Men and women can join and submit requests. The only requirement is a willingness to pray and to be prayed for.

No TTP registration required. You do not need to be part of an arm or a triad to be part of the Prayer Network. Submit a request and you are in.

How It Works
Four steps, three times a week
01
Submit your request
Use the form below. Include your name or submit anonymously. Add the request in English or Romanian -- both are supported.
02
Requests are reviewed and added to the list
Each request is read and confirmed before being included. Active requests remain on the list until they are resolved or the submitter asks for them to be removed.
03
The network receives the email
Monday, Wednesday, and Friday at 6:00 AM Bucharest time, every member of the network receives the full list of active prayer points.
04
The network prays
That is it. The power is not in the system. It is in a group of people who actually pray.
Anonymity
Your name is optional

When you submit a request, you can choose to remain anonymous. The request will appear in the email as submitted -- with no name attached. The network prays for the need, not the narrative.

What You Are Committing To
Simple. Steady.
  • Receive three emails per week with the active prayer points
  • Pray for the requests on the list
  • Submit your own requests when you have them
