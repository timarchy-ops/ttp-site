The Relational Core

The Triad

Three men. Three positions. A structure built for formation, not fellowship. The relational engine at the heart of TTP.

"And the things you have heard me say in the presence of many witnesses entrust to reliable men who will also be qualified to teach others."
2 Timothy 2:2
Foundation
Men form men

Iron sharpens iron. That is not a metaphor for motivation or accountability groups. It is a description of a process that is directional, sustained, and costly. Iron presses against iron. There is friction. There is heat. Something changes.

The triad is built on a simple recognition: every man who wants to grow needs someone who has walked further, someone who is walking beside him, and someone he is responsible for bringing along. Remove any one of those three and the formation is incomplete.

Three Positions
Above. Across. Below.
I
Above
The Mentor
The man who has walked further and chooses to walk back. He does not manage you. He speaks from experience, asks the questions you are avoiding, and holds the longer view when you can only see the month you are in.
II
Across
The Battle Buddy
The man at the same level, in the same season. He is not your cheerleader. He is the one who calls you on the gap between what you say you want and how you actually live. No distance between you.
III
Below
The Mentee
The man you are responsible for. This position completes the structure. Teaching is formation. Carrying someone else forces you to consolidate what you actually know and to live what you are asking him to live.
Triads are movement-wide. Geography does not determine who sharpens you. The man above you may be in another city. The man beside you may work across town. What matters is the relationship, not the proximity.

How You Enter
The arm comes first

Triads are not assigned. They are formed through sustained contact and earned trust. The arm is where that trust is built. Men who are running together, playing football together, or fishing together for months begin to know each other at a level that makes real formation possible.

The triad asks for approximately a year of maturation. It is not a structure you opt into on day one. It is something you grow toward. The arm is the door.
