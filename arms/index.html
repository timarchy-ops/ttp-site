The Entry Point

The Arms

Activity-based micro-groups built around what men are already doing. The arm is where trust is built. Trust is what makes everything else possible.

"As iron sharpens iron, so one person sharpens another."
Proverbs 27:17
The Problem
Every man's calendar is already full

Men do not lack activity. They lack men to do it with who know them well enough to matter. The arm starts where men already are. It does not ask them to add another meeting to their week. It asks them to do what they are already doing, with other men, consistently enough that something real develops.

An arm is a small group of 4 to 8 men, organized around a shared activity. The activity is not the point. The relationships the activity produces are.

Monthly Rhythm
Two gatherings. One purpose.
Monthly Social
Coffee or a meal
Time outside the activity. A table, some food, a longer conversation. This is where men go from acquaintances to something more. No agenda. No format. Just time.
Monthly Spiritual
Bible study or prayer
One gathering per month that goes deeper. Scripture, prayer, and the kind of honesty that only surfaces when men have already built some trust. The arm leader sets the direction.
Arm Leader
What it asks of you

An arm does not run itself. It needs a man willing to hold the space: set the rhythm, invite others in, and show up consistently enough that the group becomes something the men can count on.

  • 01Organize the monthly social and the monthly spiritual gathering
  • 02Invite men into the arm and maintain a group of 4 to 8
  • 03Show up consistently and model what the arm is about
  • 04Stay connected to the broader TTP structure and bring what you are learning back to your arm
